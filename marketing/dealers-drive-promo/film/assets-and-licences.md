No third-party photograph, stock image or AI-image service was used. No
Cars24, Spinny or other marketplace asset was scraped, copied or referenced
beyond the brief's description of the kind of photography wanted. The car
drawings carry no manufacturer logos; number plates show a plain
"Dealers-Drive" mask; dealership fascias are blank colour bands.

These are **stylised placeholders**, chosen because no image generation was
available when the film was made. To use real photography, see "Replacing
them with real photography" in the README.

## People and data shown

All names, phone numbers, email addresses, GSTINs, PANs and addresses in the
film are fictional development seed data. Phone numbers are in the block
`+91 90000 1xxxx`–`3xxxx`; emails use `example.com`. The dealer profile's
contact block and the portfolio's map panel are deliberately kept out of
frame. No real customer, dealer or administrator data, credential, token or
API key appears.
