## Retention, deletion, security and documents (applies to each row)

CONFIRMED IN CODE: no general personal-data retention scheduler or customer erasure workflow. Sessions expire/revoke, OTP tickets expire and replay counters expire, documents/images are deleted on permitted replacement/removal, never-submitted drafts may be deleted, and merged users are marked DELETED while retained. These are lifecycle controls, not a complete privacy erasure process. FK cascade definitions do not constitute an operational erasure policy.

BUSINESS CONFIRMATION REQUIRED: database/backups/object-store/provider/log retention and geography, legal holds, whether document data can be masked, staff need-to-know practice. LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED: approve category-specific schedule with responsible owner, statutory exceptions, provider deletion, backup expiry and evidence minimization before publication. Do not adopt invented 30/90-day promises. New evidence has no names, phones, emails, raw IP, UA, signed URLs, tokens or document content; store actor/subject UUID, version/digest/context and server timestamp only. Retention deletion is a reviewed privileged maintenance operation, never an application API.

Security: opaque hashed HttpOnly sessions, OAuth state/nonce/PKCE, server-verifiable OTP/replay defence, strict request schemas, per-tenant checks, transactional role reauthorization, private signed document reads, request rate limits and log redaction. No claim of universal encryption at rest or security certification is substantiated. Free-text complaints, descriptions, notes, filenames and enquiry messages may contain sensitive content; collection notices instruct users to omit unrelated personal/financial/medical data. Review logging and outbound mail for incidental sensitive content.

Documents: Terms covers accounts; Privacy covers all categories; Dealer Agreement covers business/KYC/staff; Listing Policy covers vehicles/photos; Grievance covers support; Data Rights covers correction/withdrawal/erasure request process. Separate cookie page is unnecessary: necessary cookies/storage and third-party embeds are described in Privacy.
