# Legal release and privacy operations

Status: REVIEW_REQUIRED. This change is suitable for engineering and Indian legal counsel review, not production publication. The application implements choices and records; it does not establish statutory compliance, appoint an officer or replace an operational privacy programme.

## Version control and approval

The canonical documents and contextual notices live in `packages/contracts/src/legal-documents/v1.ts`. The generated `DRAFT_DOCUMENTS.md` is a review copy. After building contracts, run `node scripts/export-legal-documents.mjs` to regenerate it. Snapshot hashes in the contracts tests detect unversioned edits. Once effective, retain the old source and archive entry permanently; amendments require a new version, new immutable snapshot and explicit decisions about effective date and renewed agreement. Never update old receipt versions, digests or timestamps to manufacture acceptance.

The release predicate requires the source approval flag, every current document's review status, an assigned effective date and a version without `draft`. Before changing those values, resolve `OPEN_LEGAL_DECISIONS.md`, complete the factual text and obtain owner and qualified Indian counsel approval of the exact snapshots. Retain dated review evidence identifying the versions, document hashes, contracting entity, officer, applicable jurisdictions and commencement assumptions. Approval metadata is a deliberate code gate; it cannot verify that human review actually happened.

The six public pages and archive routes are dynamic. Draft pages have no indexing permission or sitemap entries and return 404 in a production runtime. API startup refuses draft collection when `APP_ENV=production`, or when `NODE_ENV=production` has no explicit application environment. The web additionally recognises Vercel's production environment. Explicit local/preview environments can collect synthetic draft evidence for testing.

## Controlled rollout

Keep `LEGAL_ENFORCEMENT_ENABLED=false` on both applications in production for this PR. No production migration, merge or deployment has been performed or requested by this change. A later approved release must apply the additive migration before enabling collection, deploy matching contract versions to API and web, verify both flags and approve activation separately. Unknown fields and stale versions are rejected. Legal payloads presented to a disabled API are rejected rather than silently ignored, which exposes mismatched rollout configuration.

The migration adds `legal_events` and a nullable enquiry withdrawal timestamp. Existing rows receive no fabricated acceptance. With collection disabled, existing workflows continue without requiring receipts. With collection enabled, existing users can sign in and access drafts and support, but protected enquiry/save/submission actions require current personal Terms. Dealer-facing lead access and submission require current dealership agreement. Only an active OWNER who expressly declares authority may bind a dealership; role assignment alone is not legal proof of authority. Managers and staff may perform their existing operational actions after applicable checkpoints, but cannot accept on behalf of the business.

For assisted onboarding, a salesperson's OTP cooperation or document collection does not bind a dealer. The dealer must claim the dealership and an authorized owner must accept the agreement before verification submission or approval. An assisted listing needs an owner declaration for the exact current vehicle contents and next submission number. Editing material fields invalidates that declaration. Reviewers must request changes and obtain a fresh declaration when required. This adds no V2 inspection or mechanical/title certification.

## Evidence and access

Receipts use authenticated actor identifiers, server time, subject scope, action, context, version and SHA-256 digest. Personal Terms acceptance, Privacy acknowledgement, dealership acceptance, enquiry permission, withdrawal and listing declaration are distinct events. No IP address, user agent, email, phone, document bytes or token is copied into this table. Unique event keys make retries idempotent. Listing contexts bind the declaration to a material-field fingerprint and submission cycle. Historical versions remain addressable even after a new version is introduced.

Authenticated people can inspect their own recent history; absorbed account identifiers remain discoverable after identity merges without rewriting the original events. Admin access is read-only, requires `admin:audit:read` and a known UUID plus subject type; sales staff cannot query it. Customer responses and history use `no-store`. Normal UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE on the event table are rejected by database triggers. A privileged table owner could disable triggers or change DDL: this is not cryptographic protection against a database administrator. Before production, approve least-privilege database roles, privileged maintenance procedure, retention/legal holds, export custody and counsel's electronic-record evidentiary requirements.

## Privacy requests and grievances

The data-rights page routes access, correction and deletion requests to the existing Account Issue support workflow, with a Privacy request subject. It does not require renewed Terms. Unauthenticated people retain the contact/support channel. Operators must verify identity proportionately, track requests, communicate exceptions and complete changes through authorized procedures. No automated erasure scheduler, statutory consent-manager integration or guarantee of provider/backups deletion has been introduced.

An authenticated customer can withdraw permission for future Platform disclosure on a selected enquiry, including an older enquiry linked from their history. The transaction checks ownership and records withdrawal once. Subsequent dealer inbox responses mask name, phone and message; dashboard previews omit withdrawn enquiries and pending email workers skip them. A delivery already in flight or previously delivered email/contact cannot be recalled. Dealership records outside the Platform must be addressed with the dealership and evaluated against lawful retention grounds. Withdrawal does not falsely erase an enquiry, change its commercial status or delete required dispute evidence.

Before release, appoint the relevant grievance officer and, if applicable, nodal officer, publish verified monitored contacts and establish escalation and coverage while the sole operator is unavailable. Counsel must determine classification and applicable time limits: the e-commerce framework generally uses 48-hour acknowledgement and one-month redress; the intermediary framework has different acknowledgement/disposal clocks and accelerated categories. Do not treat one workflow as satisfying every statutory regime. Maintain a dated register, reminders, decisions, reasons for retention/refusal and appeal/referral information without collecting unnecessary identity documents.

## Retention, providers and incidents

Approve category-specific retention for identities, sessions/OTP proofs, dealership KYC, listing media, enquiries, support, consent evidence, audit and security logs. Record the trigger, duration, legal basis, hold process, deletion/anonymisation method and provider/backups coverage. Existing technical TTLs are not an approved comprehensive legal schedule. Evaluate statutory transaction and electronic evidence requirements and CERT-In's applicable log preservation/incident reporting directions without treating a consent checkbox as security compliance.

Confirm active Google, MSG91, Resend, R2/S3, Vercel/AWS and optional logging configurations, processing regions, service agreements, access controls and erasure capabilities against actual deployment. Google Maps embeds now load only after the visitor selects the map button; essential authentication/storage is explained in the Privacy notice. No optional marketing system or tracking-consent banner is invented. Adding analytics, marketing, payments, paid placement, V2 inspections or new vendors requires a fresh data-flow and legal review.

Define an incident owner, evidence preservation process, access revocation, provider escalation, applicable CERT-In reporting and future DPDP breach-notification preparation. Counsel must recheck staged DPDP commencement and 2026 e-commerce amendments near the actual effective date. Drafting and local tests do not demonstrate operational readiness of external providers or production controls.
