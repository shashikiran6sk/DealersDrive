# Two policies and dealership sharing

Git is authoritative for exact policy text. `legal-documents/v1.ts` is retained byte-for-byte from unmerged PR #279; no previous identifier or hash is relabeled. `v2.ts` composes its clauses into two independently versioned review drafts, Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy. Dealer, listing and grievance sections use stable anchors; old routes redirect permanently. `/data-rights` is a functional privacy control page, not a third policy. Public browsing requires no account; production drafts are blocked and unindexed. The original grievance disclosures and contextual authentication, document and vehicle notices remain.

PostgreSQL `legal_policy_versions` stores immutable version metadata/hash and publication state. These migrations register DRAFT snapshots only. Source approval, review status and effective date remain false/null. A later reviewed publication must insert a NEW approved, non-draft immutable version with dated approval reference and effective date, deploy the matching Git archive and enable the flags deliberately. Production acceptance validates that PostgreSQL metadata agrees with the source hash and effective publication state. No CMS, admin editor or object storage for policy content is introduced.

Account registration uses one unticked sentence and working Terms/Privacy links; it records separate ACCEPT and ACKNOWLEDGE events. Privacy acknowledgment is not permission for all processing. Dealer draft creation, OTP and document upload create no contractual receipt. Complete Review & Submit validates active owner membership inside the transaction, records authority plus current consolidated Terms once and transitions to review atomically. A failed submission rolls back evidence. Existing active/pending/suspended dealers have an owner checkpoint on My agreements; drafts are directed to Review & Submit. Managers and representatives cannot bind the owner. No legacy acceptance is inferred from status, OTP, verification or an old Dealer Agreement.

Assisted creation preserves #301's verified owner account provisioning: authenticated representatives work on their own drafts in approved service locations; phone/email proofs belong to the owner. The owner signs in with the existing Google/phone flow, reviews the current application and submits. The salesperson receives no owner session and cannot provide an agreement to the sales submit API. Standard approval, manual activation, reinstatement, listing submission/moderation and dealer lead access require current dealership Terms when enforcement is enabled. These gates do not waive separate verification requirements or rewrite dealership statuses. Representative document commits retain their SALES actor audit attribution.

`contact_sharing_events` is an append-only grant/withdrawal history. Grant scope is authenticated customer + listing-derived dealership + fixed purpose + ordered data categories + independently versioned notice/hash. Identity is not inferred from typed contacts, browser storage or cookies without authenticated server verification. There is no anonymous enquiry API: unverified callers receive 401. Account merges do not silently expand consent; current canonical customer identity must grant separately, while a verified merged identity may withdraw its historical grant.

All enquiry submissions read the available listing and lock both listing and vehicle before validating matching ownership. The supplied expected recipient is compared with that current ownership; it never determines where the enquiry goes. A changed dealership requires review and separate permission. A customer/dealership advisory transaction lock serializes grants, withdrawal and reuse. Concurrent first enquiries create one effective grant; the existing per-customer/listing lock rejects repeated open enquiries without duplicate evidence or notifications. Every enquiry needs an explicit Submit action and records a DISCLOSE receipt associated with its grant. Reading permission status never creates an enquiry or disclosure.

A grant is usable only with matching purpose/categories/notice/hash, no withdrawal and no expiry. Unrelated Terms updates do not alter the sharing-notice version. Material changes require a newly reviewed notice/scope and renewed choice; no old grant is expanded. All grants are evidence, not mutable current-state flags. Unique withdrawal references make repeated withdrawals idempotent. Both a dealership control and the historical enquiry control withdraw the associated grant and mark every related enquiry atomically. Historical enquiries, events and timestamps remain. Re-grant creates a new event; it does not restore access to earlier withdrawn enquiries.

Dealer inbox mapping and dashboard selection suppress contact/message details for revoked, expired, insufficient or missing grants when enforcement is active. Pending notification rendering checks both the grant and dealership agreement. Already delivered or in-flight external messages cannot be recalled; operators must handle dealership-held copies and lawful retention separately. This limitation is stated in the Privacy Policy and withdrawal UI, not disguised as erasure.

Privacy controls and support requests remain available without new Terms acceptance. Evidence contains actor IDs, server timestamps and hashes, never copied phone/email/IP/user-agent/OTP/file content. History is private, bounded and no-store; admin access requires audit permission and a known subject. Structured errors permit monitoring rejected permission, recipient-change, publication and owner-acceptance operations without logging personal data. Privileged database administrators can alter DDL; append-only triggers do not guarantee administrator-proof cryptographic custody.

Directions use validated business coordinates first, then a complete public business address encoded through URLSearchParams. An incomplete address uses an existing validated Google share link if available; otherwise the control is absent with an explanation. The public iframe is removed and `embedUrl` stays null for response compatibility. Stored map links, geocoding, address verification and internal location management remain.

Disabling new collection does not bypass known sharing history: a withdrawn, expired or insufficient grant still blocks a new disclosure until fresh permission can be collected. Submitted collection payloads are rejected while collection is disabled. Existing valid grants remain enforced.

## Compatibility and rollback

Apply additive migrations before deploying matching API/web/contracts, keeping LEGAL_ENFORCEMENT_ENABLED=false in both production applications. Nullable enquiry columns do not backfill acceptance or sharing. New table indexes and concurrent enquiry grant index avoid a production-wide data rewrite; nullable foreign key validation is separated. Constraints and immutability changes require short DDL locks: rehearse on a production-shaped sandbox, use a deployment lock/statement timeout and observe blocked transactions. Prisma migrations are forward-only; do not drop evidence tables or reverse hashes.

Inventory deployed policy versions, receipts and legacy dealer statuses through an authorized read-only operational process before enabling enforcement. No production database was accessed for this implementation. Existing dealers must complete the owner checkpoint rather than acquire fabricated V2 acceptance. Drafts retain their business/documents/identity state and continue to final review. An incomplete historical receipt remains incomplete. Rollout flags remain off until counsel and business approvals, operational contacts, retention/custody and provider checks are recorded.

Rollback to a release that understands withdrawal/grants. Do not revert to pre-consent main with new receipts present: its inbox/email code would ignore withdrawal. During an incident, disable new enquiry submission and lead delivery at the existing deployment layer and retain grant-aware masking; restore the prior compatible release. A flag being disabled is not authority to disclose withdrawn grants: masking and withdrawal continue for recorded grants. Preserve databases/migrations and exact Git snapshots. Reconcile in-flight external sends using delivery logs without replaying suppressed jobs.

## Baseline #309 and independently versioned Privacy

PR #309 landed on main during implementation. Its Vercel Web Analytics integration is retained for public traffic. The adapter excludes authentication, account, dealer workspace, admin, sales and private support paths; its beforeSend boundary discards URL queries/fragments and custom events. Private route navigation is checked again before transmission even when the SDK was loaded on a preceding public page. This does not assert that all provider request metadata is outside personal-data law, or substitute for a lawful-ground/processor review.

Terms remains `2.0-draft.1`; the analytics-aware Privacy draft is `2.0-draft.2` in `v3.ts`. The previous `v2.ts` Privacy snapshot and hash remain immutable. Current document rendering and digesting select each document's own version. The affirmative account control carries the displayed Privacy version; ACKNOWLEDGE evidence records that actual version. A stale Privacy version is rejected independently of Terms. Account settings show a current-notice acknowledgment checkpoint without treating it as permission for analytics. Dealership sharing stays on its unchanged, separately scoped `2.0-draft.1` notice: the privacy amendment neither expands nor revokes it. PostgreSQL registers the new Privacy draft as a ninth historical snapshot, not as an approved publication.

Provider facts were checked against [Vercel's official privacy documentation](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy). Confirm hosting-side collection, regions, agreements, referrer/request metadata handling and applicable notice/choice requirements with the operator and Indian counsel before release. No analytics consent or legal compliance is inferred from account acknowledgment.
