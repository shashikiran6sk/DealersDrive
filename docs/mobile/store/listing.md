Find used cars from independent dealerships with Dealers-Drive. Browse vehicle photographs, prices and specifications, search by make or model, and narrow results by location and vehicle preferences.

Explore dealership profiles and available inventory. Sign in with your verified Indian mobile number to save cars, manage enquiries and follow support conversations across the app and website. Choose whether to share your contact details with each dealership through the enquiry flow, and manage future sharing in your privacy controls.

Your profile includes policies, account controls and a separate account-deletion request path. Dealers-Drive helps you discover vehicles and connect with dealerships; check the vehicle, documents and seller details before making a purchase.
